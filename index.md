I develop human-interpretable AI systems that translate complex biomedical data into actionable biological insights.

I combine LLM-based data curation with multimodal AI training to enable single-cell transcriptomics analysis through an interactive chat interface ([CellWhisperer](https://cellwhisperer.bocklab.org/), 2025, *Nature Biotechnology*), which I extended to histopathology analysis ([SpatialWhisperer](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Ze7U293Zw4), *ICML* 2026; [SpotWhisperer](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.07.14.664402), 2025, *bioRxiv*). I leverage this multimodal AI to reason over large B-cell lymphoma patients using AI agents, building on my earlier work on text-based biomedical simulation with LLMs ([SimulateGPT](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compbiomed.2024.108796), 2024, *Computers in Biology and Medicine*).

My research at the intersection of mechanistic biology and AI methods development draws on an interdisciplinary background, combining a Ph.D. in Systems Biology from ETH Zurich focused on [gene regulation in embryonic development](https://doi.org/10.15252/embr.202254762) (2022, *EMBO Reports*) with Bachelor's and Master's studies in Computer Science and AI at TU Berlin ([PAVOOC](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/bty935), 2018, *Bioinformatics*).
